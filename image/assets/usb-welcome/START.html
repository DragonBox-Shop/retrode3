Welcome to Retrode3

Open retrode3.local or, in local USB mode, 172.31.255.1.

When Internet sharing is enabled on the computer before connecting, Retrode3 accepts its DHCP settings.

The Retrode3 exposes a USB NCM network connection. Your computer can obtain an address automatically via DHCP. If not, configure 172.31.255.2 with netmask 255.255.255.248 on the USB network adapter.

This welcome disk is read-only and does not contain your ROMs. Cartridge dumps are accessed through the web interface and, once configured, through Samba over the USB network.

USB serial is available as a separate CDC ACM interface. SSH is optional and is not needed for normal Retrode3 operation.
